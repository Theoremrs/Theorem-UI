# Field notes: the north headland survey

Three walks along the headland between October and November, recording where
the path has eroded and which markers survive.

## Findings

- The cliff edge moved inland by roughly two metres at the second stile.
- Marker posts 4 and 7 are gone; 5 has been re-set by the ranger.
- The lower path floods at high spring tides.

| 4      | missing  | 14 Oct    |
| 5      | re-set   | 9 Nov     |
