Two virtual chemicals: A feeds in everywhere, B consumes it (A + 2B → 3B) and decays at the kill rate. Both diffuse, B slower than A. Tiny changes in feed/kill move you between spots, stripes, mazes and waves — try nudging a slider while a pattern is growing.
